# History Forge (demo)

History-Forge demo prototype, © 2026 E. Yıldırım. The portal developed in the project will be released as open source. This demo itself: see LICENSE.

**Open:** https://the-arkitekk.github.io/history-forge-demo/ — the first visit downloads about 260 MB (model, video, sound); after that it also works offline.
- **Quest 3:** open the link in the Meta Quest Browser, wait for the model, press **Enter VR**. Wear headphones. Push a thumbstick forward and release to jump; step into the yellow spheres to watch the VR180 videos (B / Y leaves a video).
- **Phone or tablet:** open the link, drag to look or tap the round button to look by moving the device, use the on-screen stick to walk, tap a sphere to enter a video. Headphones recommended.
- **Laptop:** open the link, WASD or arrow keys to walk, drag to look, click a sphere (or press Enter next to it) to enter a video.
- **Help:** the **?** button in the corner explains the controls and symbols.

**Türkçe:** Bağlantıyı açın; ilk açılışta yaklaşık 260 MB iner. Quest 3'te Meta Quest Tarayıcı ile açıp **Enter VR**'a basın; telefonda sürükleyerek bakın, ekrandaki çubukla yürüyün; dizüstünde WASD ile yürüyüp küreye tıklayın. Kulaklık önerilir. Yardım için köşedeki **?** düğmesi.
